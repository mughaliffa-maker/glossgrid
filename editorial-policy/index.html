Trust

Editorial Policy

Original value

Every indexable design or collection should contribute useful information beyond an image, such as a salon brief, shape and length compatibility, technique, finish, maintenance considerations or meaningful variations.

Accuracy

Design attributes should be assigned by a human editor who can inspect the manicure. Do not claim a specific product, technique, artist or trend source without evidence.

AI-assisted work

AI may support drafting, organization or tagging, but final descriptions should be reviewed for visual accuracy, physical feasibility, repetition and usefulness. AI-generated images should be clearly managed and must not be presented as the work of a real nail artist.

Updates and corrections

Material corrections should be made promptly. Update dates should change only when the page receives a meaningful review or revision.

Commercial relationships

Sponsored and affiliate content must be clearly disclosed. Payment should not guarantee a positive conclusion or undisclosed ranking advantage.
