Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026

This is a practical starter template, not jurisdiction-specific legal advice. Update it for the analytics, advertising, email and consent tools you actually install.

Information stored on your device

The demo uses browser local storage to remember saved nail designs and a demonstration email submission. This information remains in the browser unless you clear it.

Analytics and advertising

The starter package does not include active Google Analytics, Pinterest Tag or AdSense code. When these services are added, this policy must identify the providers, data collected, purposes, retention and user controls.

Cookies and consent

Before activating advertising or non-essential analytics, add an appropriate consent mechanism for the countries you serve. Do not claim that the site uses no cookies after installing services that use cookies or similar technologies.

Contact

Replace the demo contact address with your operational privacy email before launch.
