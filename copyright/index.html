Rights and attribution

Image & Copyright Policy

Publish only images you created, generated under suitable usage terms, licensed, commissioned or received with explicit permission. Keep proof of rights for every collection.

Accepted image sources

  • Photography created and owned by the website.
  • Work commissioned under a written commercial-use agreement.
  • Nail-artist or user submissions with explicit publication permission.
  • Properly licensed stock or generated media used within its license terms.

Attribution records

Maintain a private content-rights register containing the image filename, creator, source URL, license or permission evidence, date obtained, required credit and allowed uses.

Removal requests

A rights holder should be able to submit the affected URL, ownership evidence and requested resolution. Valid requests should be documented and handled quickly.

Do not scrape social platforms

Do not download high-performing images from Pinterest, Instagram, TikTok or salon portfolios merely because they are publicly visible. Public visibility is not permission to republish commercially.
