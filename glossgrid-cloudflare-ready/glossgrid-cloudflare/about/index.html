About the project

Nail inspiration should be easier to use.

GlossGrid is a launch-ready demo. Replace its sample images, email address and brand details with your own original or properly licensed content before applying for AdSense.

What GlossGrid does

GlossGrid is designed as a visual nail-idea library rather than a traditional generic blog. Visitors can browse by color, shape, length, season, occasion and style, save designs without creating an account, and copy a concise salon brief.

Editorial purpose

Every published design should explain the visible manicure accurately, identify practical attributes and help a visitor decide whether the look suits their preferred length, shape and occasion.

Who it serves

The platform is intended for salon customers, nail technicians and people recreating manicures at home. Content should be clear enough for a customer and specific enough to support a useful conversation with a professional.
